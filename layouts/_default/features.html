Nanolathe uses TA’s original models and textures, with a new renderer that adds zoom, lighting and other effects. You can compare the results below, including a few effects you can turn on and off individually.

Modern GPU is the default. Press F10 in-game to switch to Classic, or use Options → Nanolathe. The display settings don’t change the gameplay rules.

Terrain and tree detail is synthesized locally from your game files in the native beta. The browser demo uses the original terrain.
