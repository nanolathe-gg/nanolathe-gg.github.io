The project

Keep the game.
Rebuild the engine.

Total Annihilation gave us sprawling battlefields, mechanical armies, and a very particular shade of green. Nanolathe is rebuilding the engine that makes it all move.

Original file formats. Documented behavior. A modern codebase you can read, run, and help improve. Single-player skirmish and campaign are the focus; development is ongoing.
